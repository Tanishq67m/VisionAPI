# VisionStream — Story & Positioning

*Our marketing source of truth. Everything on the site should ladder up to this.*

## One sentence
VisionStream is the **perception layer for AI agents** — one call returns a cleaned screenshot *and* the page's real structure (every element boxed and labeled), so an agent can **see** any interface and act on it.

## The category we're quietly against
The default way to give an agent the web is to turn pages into **clean text** — markdown for a model to *read*. That's the right tool when the job is reading: research, RAG, summarizing, Q&A over content. The leaders there are great at it. We are not trying to be a better text-scraper.

## The gap we exist for
Agents are moving from *reading* the web to *operating* it — logging in, clicking, filling forms, checking a dashboard, confirming a price on screen. Text throws away exactly what that needs: **where things are, what's interactive, and what the screen actually looks like.** Markdown is a transcript. An agent that has to act on a UI needs a map.

Raw screenshots are the other half-answer — but a screenshot alone makes the model *guess*: expensive vision tokens, wrong coordinates, hallucinated buttons.

## Our answer
VisionStream gives an agent **both halves of perception**: the cleaned image **and** the structured, coordinate-grounded map of the interface — every button, form, table and input, each with a bounding box. Your agent doesn't read a description of the page. It sees the page and knows what it can act on.

## Why the name is the strategy
- **Vision** = sight / perception. Our logo is a viewfinder reticle — lean into it.
- **Stream** = live, real-time, a feed.
- VisionStream **streams vision** — live perception of any interface — to your agent.
- The brand visual is the product literally *seeing*: a page with its elements lighting up in boxes. That overlay IS the identity.

## Why choose us (honest, no names)
- The text-first tools optimize for **reading** the web. We optimize for **seeing and acting on** it.
- For agents that operate interfaces, text isn't enough and raw pixels are wasteful. That exact gap is our whole product.
- We're not racing to "scrape more of the web." We're the layer that lets your agent **perceive an interface and ground its actions** in what's actually on screen.
